A human ratifies intent, dispatches one bounded work order at a time with an explicit capability grant, an agent works inside that grant, a separate review checks the result against the record, and the human accepts on evidence. Between work orders the project is locked. Everything is plain files in your repository. There is no service.

Spec tools answer what should the agent build. Writwall answers what comes after: who authorized this change, what was the agent allowed to touch, what did it try, what was actually blocked, and what did a human accept.

It ran on itself for ten work orders. Doctrine 8.3.5

Where this came from

The methodology is document control, moved from construction to software. In building automation you do not accept a smoke damper because the submittal says it closes. You put it in alarm and you watch it close. That is the birth test. You do not regenerate a plan from the as-built and call the drift resolved. You hold the plan and the state as two documents that must agree. A decision without its rejected alternatives transfers the conclusion and not the ability to judge the next case. Twenty years of that discipline is where every part of Writwall came from.

The full mapping is an essay: Document control for AI-assisted development. It is the argument in long form, and it stands on its own without the repository.

What it does not do

Stated plainly, because the methodology requires it of itself.

EnforcementThe only mechanical enforcement shipped is a Claude Code adapter. With any other agent it is instruction-bounded, and the README uses those two words. The hook also cannot fail closed on its own absence: if it does not start, it does not block.
SessionsAgents still degrade inside long sessions. The method makes sessions short and their deaths cheap. It does not make them immortal.
JudgmentA vacuous plan yields vacuous conformance. Gates check work against intent. They cannot supply intent.
EvidenceOne pilot on one repository. It shows the records exposed real drift and that tested channels blocked forbidden actions. It does not prove Writwall lowers cost or generalizes to other teams.

product, and the channel trailer is Silo's showcase.

- **Silo's line,** wherever Silo appears on GAMING's accounts: `Silo · a HLLMR GAMING product`, as plain

- **Silo's videos keep Silo's look,** from Silo's own media kit (`media-kit/silo/`): Silo's gold `#E3B23C`,
  Fraunces for titles, Hanken Grotesk for captions, and Silo's own mark. The launch videos were made in that
  kit. Their thumbnails follow that look, not this kit's template.
- **Everything else** (mods, channel news, what comes next) uses this kit's thumbnail template.
- **One gold:** Silo's gold and GAMING's Or are the same hex, `#e3b23c`, so a Silo graphic and a GAMING graphic
  can appear together. Silo's mark still never sits next to the HLLMR arms or wordmark (see above).
- **Watermark:** GAMING's shield. It is small, sits in a corner, and stays off the Silo mark.

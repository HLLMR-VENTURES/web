## About (long)

> HLLMR GAMING makes tools, mods and, in time, games of our own. It starts with Farming Simulator 25.
>
> Silo is a free, open-source mod manager for Farming Simulator 25. It reads a player's mods and game log,
> points to the likely culprit when the game crashes, and flags conflicts before launch. Get it at
> silo.hllmr.com.
>
> Our mods for Farming Simulator 25 include Realistic Market Demand, where selling prices respond to
> demand, and its buy-side companion, Input Market, where fuel, fertilizer and seed prices drift by month.
>
> What comes next: a shared modding core behind more mod managers (Euro Truck Simulator 2, American Truck
> Simulator, 7 Days to Die), and simulation games of our own, starting on mobile. Plans, not releases: no
> dates are promised.
>
